For reporters

Press kit

The facts behind the campaign, with links to the public data so you can build your own picture.

Fact sheet

  • 120 large petroleum tanks in South Portland, run by six major oil companies, sit close to homes, schools and child care.
  • State fence-line monitoring has now measured the air at the tank farms. At Citgo on Mechanic Street in Pleasantdale, the air consistently and dramatically exceeded the state standard for benzene, a well-studied carcinogen.
  • At South Portland Terminal on Lincoln Street, one reading registered 12 times the state’s ambient air quality standard, which is meant to protect people from cancer risk.

What the campaign is asking for

The Council should require better technology at the tank farms, and make public what special counsel advises about the city’s authority. The Act now page carries a message you can send.
