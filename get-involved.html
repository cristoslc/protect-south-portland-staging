Report an odor two ways

Smell My City app

Click here to download. Then, with just a few taps, record each time you smell something that seems hazardous. It's free, easy to use, and the data provides one more means of tracking the impact on our community. This data can then be used to advocate for action by regulatory agencies.

Download the app

City Odor Report form

In addition to using the Smell My City App which displays results publicly and in real-time, please also fill out the city’s Odor Report form.

This is the city’s preferred method for you to tell them about each time you smell something bad. However, the log of odor reports are not publicly available in real-time.

File the report form

Writing a letter to the editor

Sentry

Email the Editor; see the Sentry’s website for the submissions address. Letters should be no longer than 350 words and must include the writer’s name, full address, and phone number. The deadline is Monday at noon.

Forecaster

Email the Editor; see the Forecaster’s website for the submissions address. Letters should be no longer than 300 words and must include the writer’s name, full address, and daytime and evening phone numbers. The deadline is Monday at noon.

Portland Press Herald

Email the Editor, or send mail to Editor, Portland Press Herald, P.O. Box 1460, Portland, ME 04104-5009. Letters should be no longer than 300 words and include the writer’s name, address, and daytime phone number.
